Jiazhi Shu 束嘉贽

I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Yuan Liu. Previously, I studied at South China University of Technology, majoring in Software Engineering with a minor in Business Administration.

My research interests lie in 3D human reconstruction, human motion modeling, and generative video modeling. I am interested in building realistic, animatable digital humans and understanding how people move and interact with the world.
